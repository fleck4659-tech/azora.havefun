Azora Privacy

This page explains how Azora, operated by Azoraius Makes, treats information on the Mid Alpha platform. Azora is a family-friendly place to play games, style an avatar, chat, and use Aturius. It was created on June 26, 2026.

Who this is for

Azora is built for tablet and computer play. A guest can look around. A regular account can save a username, avatar, coins, and settings. If a player adds an email, it is only used to help them sign back in. Azora does not ask for a home address, phone number, or school name.

What Azora stores

  • Username and whether the account is a guest or a regular account.
  • Avatar colors, clothes, face, and other equipped items.
  • AzoraCoins, HalloCoins, bag items, and shop purchases made inside Azora.
  • Theme, logo choice, and other settings saved on that device.
  • Chat messages, friend requests, and mute choices. A mute only hides a person for you.
  • Games you open, Feed visits, and Studio or video saves you choose to keep.
  • Screen recordings you start yourself. They stay in your Azora recordings unless you download them.

What stays on your device

A lot of Azora is saved in the browser on your tablet or computer. That includes theme, logo, some game saves, and chat history. Clearing site data can remove those saves. Living Lands map saves that you download are files on your device. Azora does not read the rest of your computer.

Accounts on more than one device

If cloud save is on, Azora can keep a copy of your avatar, coins, bag, and settings under your username so another tablet or computer can look the same after you log in. That copy is tied to the username, not to a public profile of your real name.

Aturius

Aturius is the in-platform assistant. What you type is used to answer you, build a small game, make a shape picture, or play a sound test. Shape pictures are circles, boxes, and triangles. If a request is not allowed, Aturius does not show the picture. Aturius can read a reply out loud on your device. Voice is processed on the device for speech. Do not type passwords, addresses, or private family information into Aturius.

Chat, Feed, and videos

Chat can be seen by people in that world or that chat. Public Feed posts and public videos can be seen by other players. Videos have extra checks before they are posted. Azora can remove a post that breaks the family-friendly rules. Private Studio saves in My Videos are not the public Feed.

Shop and coins

AzoraCoins and event coins are for items inside Azora. Prices can change. A purchase record can show the item, the price, and the time. Azora does not sell your username to other companies. Buying coins, if that option is open, uses the payment screen you are sent to. Azora does not store full card numbers in the game files.

Games

Norm Games and Quick Games can save progress, a display name, and a simple place in the world so other players in that room can see your avatar. Plaza 1990, Living Lands, and Block Tower are played in the browser. Living Lands saves are files you name and download. They are not uploaded unless you choose to share them.

Device and connection

Azora checks screen size, pointer type, and whether the browser can draw 3D so the layout can fit a tablet or a computer. It can also check if you are online so a missing feature can show a notice. Azora does not use that check to follow you around other websites.

Cookies and local storage

Azora uses local storage for login state, theme, coins, and settings. A service file can cache the logo, pages, and sounds so the platform opens faster. You can remove that cache in the browser. Doing so may log you out of the saved session on that device.

What Azora does not do

  • It does not sell player lists.
  • It does not ask kids for private contact details in chat.
  • It does not show adult content. Family-friendly rules stay on.
  • It does not keep a recording unless you press Record.

Safety

Do not share passwords. Do not share your real name, school, or home address in chat. Use mute if you do not want to see one person's messages. Owner and moderation tools exist so Azora can review reports and remove a post or a game that should not be public.

Changes

This page can change as Azora moves through Mid Alpha. The date at the bottom of the main site stays June 26, 2026, which is the creation date. The privacy page was added after that, in October 2026.

Questions about privacy can be sent through the official Azora YouTube page linked on the home screen.
